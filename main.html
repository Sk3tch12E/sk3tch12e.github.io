About Me

My journey started in a school IT lab discovering Scratch. This was before I had the chance to learn "real" programming, but it hooked me immediately. In school, I moved through Small Basic and Visual Basic before finally landing on C#, though by then, I’d already discovered it myself through Unity.

Today, I’m a Senior Developer who thrives on technical complexity. Whether it's writing custom netcode or architecting enterprise software from scratch, I focus on building tools that solve real problems.

Outside of IDEs, you'll usually find me climbing, looking after my crested gecko, or listening to music. (Actually, I’m probably listening to music no matter what else I’m doing.)
